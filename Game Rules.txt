In this game, a circle appears at the center of the screen as the main target area. When the player presses and holds the control button, a line begins to grow outward in a straight direction at a constant linear speed. The objective is to release the hold at the perfect moment so the line wraps around the circle as accurately as possible.

The game measures how precisely the line matches the circle’s boundary. If the line is too short or too long, the accuracy score decreases. A perfectly timed release results in a clean wrap and earns the highest score. The scoring system is based on precision, timing, and alignment with the circle’s circumference.

## Rules and Regulations of the Game

1. **Game Objective**
   The player must grow and release a line so it wraps around the circle as accurately as possible.

2. **Starting the Game**

   * A circle appears on the screen.
   * The player starts line growth by pressing and holding the control button.

3. **Line Growth Mechanism**

   * The line grows continuously at a fixed linear speed while the button is held.
   * Releasing the button stops the growth instantly.

4. **Scoring Rules**

   * The score is calculated based on how closely the line matches the circle’s circumference.
   * A perfect wrap earns maximum points.
   * Overgrowth or undergrowth reduces the score proportionally.

5. **Accuracy Levels**

   * 95–100% match: Perfect
   * 80–94% match: Great
   * 60–79% match: Good
   * Below 60%: Poor

6. **Difficulty Progression**

   * Higher levels may include smaller circles, faster line growth, rotating circles, or moving targets.
   * Difficulty increases gradually after each successful round.

7. **Game Over Conditions**

   * The game ends if the player fails to meet the minimum accuracy requirement for a round.
   * Optional lives or retry chances may be included.

8. **Fair Play Rules**

   * No external timing tools or automated controls are allowed.
   * Players must rely only on reaction time and visual judgment.

9. **Winning Condition**

   * The player with the highest cumulative precision score wins or reaches the highest level.

10. **Gameplay Principles**

* The game rewards timing, concentration, and precision.
* Quick reactions and consistent accuracy are essential for success.
